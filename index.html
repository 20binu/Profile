01 / About

Academic work shaped by curiosity and purpose.

I am Binu R., an academician in the Department of Mathematics at Rajagiri School of Engineering & Technology, Kochi, Kerala.

My academic career spans teaching, departmental leadership, research supervision and mentorship. I hold a PhD in Mathematics from Mahatma Gandhi University. My research work focuses on neutrosophic algebraic structures, submodules, homomorphisms, decision-making environments and applications in medical science.

I also contribute as a reviewer for peer-reviewed international journals and guide research scholars and student internship programmes.
